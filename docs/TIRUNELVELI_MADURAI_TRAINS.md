## Station-wise Train Stoppages

### Tirunelveli Junction (TEN)
- **All trains originating/terminating here**: Full stop
- **Through trains**: Most stop (2-5 minutes)
- **Platform capacity**: 5 platforms
- **Daily passengers**: ~11,000+ per day

### Vanchi Maniyachchi Junction (MEJ)
- **Vande Bharat Express**: Usually skips
- **Express trains**: Selected stops
- **Passenger trains**: All stop
- **Junction importance**: Branch line to Tuticorin

### Kovilpatti (CVP)
- **Vande Bharat Express**: Stop (2 mins)
- **Superfast trains**: Most stop
- **Important for**: Textile industry connectivity

### Satur (SRT)
- **Passenger trains**: All stop
- **Express trains**: Limited stops
- **Small station**: Local connectivity

### Virudhunagar Junction (VPT)
- **Vande Bharat Express**: Stop (2-3 mins)
- **All express trains**: Stop
- **Major junction**: Branch lines to multiple directions
- **Industrial importance**: Fireworks industry hub

### Tirumangalam (TMQ)
- **Express trains**: Selected stops
- **Passenger trains**: All stop
- **Small town**: Agricultural connectivity

### Madurai Junction (MDU)
- **Major terminus/junction**: ALL trains stop
- **Platform capacity**: Multiple platforms
- **Daily passengers**: Very high footfall
- **Rake reversals**: Many trains reverse here
- **Strategic hub**: Connects to Chennai, Trichy, Rameswaram, Kanniyakumari
