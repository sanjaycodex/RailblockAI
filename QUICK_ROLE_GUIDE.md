# 🚀 Quick Role Guide

## ✅ Corrected Three-Role System

### 📋 Planner
**What I do:** Create plans & assign work  
**Login:** Click "Planner" button  
**Panel:** `/planner-panel`

**My Actions:**
- ✅ Create maintenance plans
- ✅ Run AI optimizer
- ✅ Check team availability
- ✅ **Assign approved plans to departments** (Civil, Electrical, etc.)
- ❌ Cannot approve plans

---

### 🛡️ Admin
**What I do:** Inspect & approve plans  
**Login:** Click "Admin" button  
**Panel:** `/admin-panel`

**My Actions:**
- ✅ **Review all plans**
- ✅ **Approve plans** ⭐
- ✅ **Reject plans** with concerns
- ✅ Manage users
- ❌ Typically don't create/assign (oversight role)

---

### 👁️ Viewer
**What I do:** View only (no actions)  
**Login:** Click "Viewer" button  
**Panel:** `/viewer-panel`

**My Actions:**
- ✅ View all plans
- ✅ Monitor dashboards
- ✅ Access reports
- ❌ **Cannot approve**
- ❌ **Cannot assign**
- ❌ **Completely read-only**

---

## 🔄 Workflow (3 Steps)

```
1. PLANNER creates plan → Status: PROPOSED

2. ADMIN approves plan → Status: APPROVED

3. PLANNER assigns to department → Status: ASSIGNED
```

---

## 🎯 Quick Actions

### Planner: "How do I assign work?"
1. Wait for Admin approval
2. Go to Planner Panel
3. Find approved plan
4. Click "Assign" button
5. Select department (Civil/Electrical/etc.)

### Admin: "How do I approve?"
1. Go to Admin Panel
2. See "Pending Approvals" section
3. Review plan details
4. Click "Approve Plan"

### Viewer: "What can I do?"
- View everything
- Monitor progress
- Access reports
- **No approval or assignment rights**
